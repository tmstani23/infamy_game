# Roadmap

## Tiny version (Settled scope)

- One **waterfront district at night**.
- The **hero**, plus up to **3 recruits**.
- Three enterable buildings: **teahouse**, **warehouse**, **gambling den**.
- One **AI rival clan**.
- Goal: **take and hold the gambling den**.

## Next steps

1. Decide the art pipeline and whether the camera rotates (see `art_direction.md`).
2. Placeholder art is fine: build the tiny version and find out if it is fun before investing in art.
3. Hero movement and camera in the district.
4. Recruiting and commanding crew.
5. Interiors from templates for the three buildings.
6. Rival clan AI and the den fight.
7. Then: inventory, stash, fence, documents.

Multiplayer, persistent worlds and night raid rules come after the tiny version proves fun.
