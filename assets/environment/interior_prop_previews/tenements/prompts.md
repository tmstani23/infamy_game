# Tenement furnishing concepts for Gemini

The current tenement sleeping-bay layout and shoji screens are approved. These three props replace the most visibly dated bedding, common tables, and household storage. Generate **one four-view sheet per prompt**; do not generate a room scene. Attach `game/art/interior/shop_textile_shelf.png` as the painted-style reference and the approved tenement screenshot as the camera-angle reference. The screenshot supplies perspective only; the output needs genuine transparency.

Each sheet should show the **same object** at the same scale with the camera fixed and the object rotated 90 degrees between views. Use a clean 2×2 arrangement: front/right, front/left, back/right, back/left. Give each view generous transparent margins, with no labels or panel borders. Keep upright posts vertical and long floor-contact edges parallel to one of the screenshot's ±24.1° wall/floor diagonals. We can cut out and calibrate the views individually before any game placement.

## 1. Lived-in futon — highest priority

> Make a four-view transparent game-prop sheet of **one narrow working-class Japanese futon**, laid out for sleeping on a tatami floor, for a dark 1870s port-town tenement. A thin patched indigo cotton quilt with a faded brown lining, a modest buckwheat pillow at the head, slightly uneven hand-stitched seams, and restrained signs of daily use. It should feel affordable and human, neither filthy nor luxurious. No wooden bedframe, person, floor, loose detached items, or cast shadow. Match the reference's muted semi-realistic painted finish and restrained warm upper-left light. Fixed 2:1 isometric camera; four 90° object rotations in a clean 2×2 sheet, identical design and scale, isolated on genuine alpha transparency. Long edges follow the appropriate ±24.1° floor diagonal; no perspective warping, labels, border, text, or watermark.

The deployed silhouette should fit the current narrow two-by-three-unit sleeping bay, with the pillow visibly marking the head. Different quilt colors can be tinted in-game later; one coherent base design is enough now.

## 3. Domestic storage shelf

The back should be visually finished enough for a freestanding view, while the front remains the useful storage face. Match the reference's muted semi-realistic painted finish and restrained warm upper-left light. Fixed 2:1 isometric camera; four 90° object rotations in a clean 2×2 sheet, identical design and scale, isolated on genuine alpha transparency. Base and shelf edges follow the appropriate ±24.1° floor diagonal; posts stay vertical. No room, floor, people, cast shadow, labels, border, readable text, or watermark.

Keep it narrow enough to sit against a wall near a sleeping bay without blocking the walkway. We can use it in place of some generic wall drawers after the art is approved.
