Saved prompts for Gemini / ChatGPT, copied word for word from the chat with Tim.

## How to keep the style consistent

- Attach the matching earlier image as a reference: **hero1.png** for characters, **buildings1.png** for buildings and props, **textures1.png** for textures. Then paste the saved prompt below with the changes you want.
- Shared style keywords:
  - 1870s Japan
  - Dota 2 base with anime touches (never name a game in a prompt; Gemini refuses)
  - cool and sleek
  - muted and moody, not bright, boxy or dull
  - buildings and props: isometric 2:1 angle, same lighting every time
  - textures: top-down, seamless tiles

> Semi-realistic painted illustration, muted moody colours, soft overcast light from the upper left,

**Shared prompt:**

> Semi-realistic painted game illustration for 1870s Japan, muted olive greens, weathered grey stones and brown earth, soft overcast lighting from the upper left, matching the supplied game pine and lake-shore reference. STRICT ORTHOGRAPHIC TOP-DOWN camera at 90 degrees, looking straight down, NOT an isometric standing cutout. A modular narrow lake-bank strip on a genuine transparent PNG, 1024 × 1024. Use a nominal human height of 75 pixels for scale: individual pebbles 4–12 pixels, small bank rocks 15–35 pixels, occasional reed clumps 30–55 pixels.
